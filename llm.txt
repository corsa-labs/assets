# Corsa

> Corsa is the next-generation compliance and risk operating system for modern fintech organizations across both fiat and crypto ecosystems.

## About

Corsa provides a unified platform for compliance, risk, operations, and security teams requiring complete, real-time visibility across financial infrastructure. The platform unifies onboarding data (KYC/KYB), accounts, wallets, transactions (fiat and on-chain), vendor signals, alerts, investigations, and case workflows into a single view.

## Key Features

- [Transaction Monitoring](https://corsa.finance): Real-time monitoring of fiat and on-chain transactions
- [AI-Powered Agents](https://corsa.finance): Embedded AI agents for alert triage, risk scoring, investigative summarization, and anomaly detection
- [Case Management](https://corsa.finance): End-to-end investigation and case workflows
- [KYC/KYB](https://corsa.finance): Unified onboarding data management
- [Vendor Integrations](https://corsa.finance): Consolidated vendor signals in one platform
